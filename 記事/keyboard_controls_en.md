# How to Play with Keyboard Controls

This guide explains the setup and basic operation methods for playing FFXI comfortably using only a keyboard (or keyboard + mouse).
* Note: This guide is written assuming the use of a "Full Keyboard" type equipped with a numpad.

---

## Introduction

Playing FFXI via keyboard can feel tricky until you get used to it, but once you master it, it offers an extremely smooth and responsive control experience.
The keyboard provides useful shortcuts that are impossible on a gamepad, allowing for lightning-fast reactions during critical moments.
Executing actions manually without having to create dedicated macros also saves valuable macro palette space. If you are looking to learn keyboard controls, give this guide a try!

---

## Recommended Keyboard Settings (Config)

* **Keyboard Type**: In the in-game menu, navigate to "Config" ➔ "Keyboard" and select "Full Keyboard".

> 【Basic Control Rules】
> In FFXI keyboard controls, virtually all actions and menu confirmations are executed with the **Enter key** (Confirm), while the **Escape key** is used for "Cancel".

---

### 1. Character Movement and Camera Controls

* **Movement & Turning**: Numpad `8` (Forward) / `2` (Backward) / `4` (Turn Left) / `6` (Turn Right)
* **Camera Movement (Up/Down/Left/Right)**: Arrow Keys
  * When weapons are drawn in combat, you can control the camera using `Shift` + Arrow Keys with **Auto-lock disengaged**.
* **Camera Zoom In / Out**: Numpad `9` (Zoom In) / `3` (Zoom Out)
* **Camera Angle Reset / Viewpoint Toggle**: Numpad `5`
  * Press once to switch to "First-Person View", and press again to return to Third-Person View.
  * Rapidly double-tap `5` to reset the camera angle behind your character facing forward.
* **Toggle Autorun**: Numpad `7`
* **Toggle Run / Walk**: Numpad `/`
* **Strafe Movement (Sidestep)**: While target-locked on an enemy, pressing Numpad `4` (Left) or `6` (Right) performs strafing movement.
* **Healing (Sit / Stand)**: Numpad `*` (when no target is selected)
* **Review Chat Log & Manually Cancel Buffs**: Numpad `+`
  * 【Reviewing Chat Logs】: Press `+` once and press `Enter` to scroll back through the left chat log. (If you don't use split chat windows, one press accesses the main chat log.) Pressing `+` again moves to the right chat window, and subsequent presses cycle through "Tell" and other chat tabs.
  * 【Manually Canceling Buffs】: Press `+` twice to place the cursor on the buff icon area at the top of your screen. Select the buff you wish to cancel using the arrow keys and press `Enter` to dismiss it.

---

### 2. Menu and Target Controls

* **Open / Close Main Menu**: Numpad `-`
* **Cycle Targets**: Numpad `0`
* **Target Lock / Unlock**: Numpad `*` (while targeting an entity)

#### Target Selection with Function Keys

Function keys are essential in combat and party play for rapidly selecting specific targets without delay.

<img src="../画像素材/images/keyboard_layout_function_highlighted.png?v=3" alt="Target Selection with Function Keys" style="max-width: 600px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1.5rem 0; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);">

* **Target Yourself**: `F1` or `F7`
* **Target Party Members**: `F2` to `F6`
  * From top to bottom: 2nd member is `F2`, 3rd is `F3`, ..., 6th is `F6`.
* **Target Nearest NPC / Monster**: `F8` or `F10`
* **Target Nearest PC (Player Character)**: `F9`
* Note: `F11` and `F12` have no default assignment and are generally unused.

### 3. Macro Execution and Chat Input

Macros form the foundation of combat and actions in FFXI. In keyboard play, the typical style involves holding down `Ctrl` or `Alt` with your fingers during battle.

<img src="../画像素材/images/keyboard_layout_left_highlighted.png?v=3" alt="Macro Execution Layout (Ctrl / Alt / Number Keys)" style="max-width: 600px; width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 1.5rem 0; border-radius: 8px; box-shadow: 0 4px 15px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);">

* **Alt Macro Execution**: `Alt` + `1` to `0` keys
  * (e.g., register attack macros on the Alt palette and fire off spells with `Alt` + `1`)
* **Ctrl Macro Execution**: `Ctrl` + `1` to `0` keys
  * (e.g., register recovery or support macros on the Ctrl palette for distinct separation)
* **Switching Macro Palettes (Pages)**: While holding `Ctrl` or `Alt`, press the `↑` or `↓` arrow keys to cycle through palettes.
* **Opening Chat Input**: Press the spacebar or Enter key to open the chat line, enter text, and press Enter to send.

## Useful Direct Shortcuts (Ctrl / Alt + Alphabet Keys)

These shortcuts allow you to open menus and switch chat channels instantly without browsing the main menu. Press `Ctrl` or `Alt` while pressing the following keys:

### 1. Direct Menu Access

* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `J`**: Job Ability Menu
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `W`**: Weapon Skill (WS) Menu
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `M`**: Magic List Menu
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `E`**: Equipment Menu
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `I`**: Item Inventory Menu

### 2. Direct Chat Channel Switching

* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `S`**: Switch to Say
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `P`**: Switch to Party
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `L`**: Switch to Linkshell 1
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `X`**: Switch to Linkshell 2
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `U`**: Switch to Unity
* **`Ctrl` or `Alt` + `R`**: Reply to Tell (Whisper)
  * Pressing `R` repeatedly cycles backwards through recent senders.
