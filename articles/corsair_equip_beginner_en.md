# Corsair Entry-level Equipment Guide

This guide is for beginners or returning players who are just starting out with Corsair.

## First Goals
1. **Rostam (Path C)**: The ultimate roll weapon. Even at Rank 1, it's very powerful.
2. **Kustawi +1**: A great sub weapon for TP and accuracy.
3. **Fomalhaut**: The go-to ranged weapon for Last Stand.
