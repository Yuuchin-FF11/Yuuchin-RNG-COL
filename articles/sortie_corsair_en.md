# Sortie Corsair Strategy Guide

This guide covers the role and positioning of a Corsair in the endgame content "Sortie."

## General Role
* Maintain Phantom Rolls (Chaos/Samurai are standard).
* Follow up on Scholar skillchains with Last Stand or Leaden Salute.
* Use Quick Draw to gain TP instantly.
