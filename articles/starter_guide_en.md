# FF11 From Scratch! The Ultimate Start Dash Guide for Complete Beginners (Up to Level 30)

## 1. Introduction: Why I wrote this article
This article was born when a viewer on my YouTube channel, who had never played FFXI before, got interested and actually started playing!

As I taught them various things, I realized there are many "traps" that are completely unknown to brand new players, even though they are common sense to experienced ones.
The starting cities in FFXI have a tutorial of sorts, but it's not very user-friendly. You're thrown into the world without any explanation of controls, combat mechanics, or what to do next. For gamers accustomed to modern games, this can feel like a "brutal design" (lol).

So, in this article, I've compiled the "modern, true tutorial" to help anyone starting FFXI from scratch navigate past the first major hurdle (acquiring a Support Job) without getting lost!

---

## 2. The First Big Trap: You must accept "Eminence Record" objectives yourself!
The very first thing you must do when you land in the city: Set your **"Eminence Record (Objectives)"**.

Unlike modern games, quests do not start automatically. If you don't open the menu and accept the objectives yourself, the tutorial will never progress.

1. Open the menu, go to **"Quests"** > **"Eminence Record"** > **"Tutorial"**.
2. Manually set the following objectives and clear them in order.

---

## 3. Aim for 10 Clears! The Ultimate "Alter Ego Chain" Route
First, aim to clear a total of **10 tutorial objectives**.
Doing them randomly is tough, so I recommend following this **"Fastest & Strongest Route (Alter Ego Chain)"**!

### [The First 5 to Accept]
Accept the following 5 from the "Basics" tutorial section:
1. **First Step Forward**
2. **Vanquish One Enemy**
3. **Heal Without Using Magic**
4. **Call for Help**
5. **Undertake a Fields of Valor Training Regime**
   * *💡 What is FoV?: It refers to the "Field Manual" book placed just outside the city. Just touch the book and accept any training regime to clear it.*

### [The Alter Ego Chain (Crucial!)]
In addition to the above, accept the objective to call an "Alter Ego (Trust NPC)". Clearing this will trigger the next objective in a chain. By simply **clearing them in the order they appear**, you'll reach 10 clears in no time. Plus, you'll instantly gather powerful allies—hitting two birds with one stone.

6. **Call an Alter Ego**
7. **Alter Ego: Valaineral**
   * ↓ (Clearing this unlocks the next)
8. **Alter Ego: Mihli Aliapoh**
   * ↓ (Clearing this unlocks the next)
9. **Alter Ego: Tenzen**
   * ↓ (Clearing this unlocks the next)
10. **Alter Ego: Adelheid**
    * ↓ (Clearing this unlocks the next)

## 4. Join a Unity and Get the Best Healer!
Once you clear 10 Eminence Records, a new objective called **"All for One"** will be added.
Accept this, speak to the **"Unity NPC (A.M.A.N. Registration)"** located near the city exits, and join a Unity (similar to an NPC clan).

* *💡 Don't know where the NPC is?: The coordinates and name are written in the description of the "All for One" objective!*

### Recommended Starting Unity: "Apururu" or "Yoran-Oran"
By joining a Unity, completing tasks, and accumulating points, you can call your Unity leader as an exclusive Alter Ego.
For beginners, it is overwhelmingly recommended to join either **Apururu** or **Yoran-Oran**, as they are both exceptional healers!

From here on out, "Unity" tasks will also be added to your Eminence Records. Set tasks that look doable and continue your adventure.

---

## 5. To the Field! Beware the "Enchantment" Mechanic
With preparations complete, head outside the city to start leveling up.
At this time, having rings that grant bonus experience points (like the Jubilee Ring or Echad Ring) is highly efficient, but **beware of their different usage methods**.

* **Jubilee Ring (Passive type)**
  Simply equipping it grants the effect.
* **Echad Ring (Enchantment type)**
  This is an **"Enchantment item"** unique to FFXI. Just equipping it does nothing; you must **"Use" it as an item while it is equipped** to activate the EXP boost.

### How to read "Charges" and "Recast Time"
Looking at the stats of an Enchantment item, you'll see markings like "[3/3]" or "[15:00]".
* **[3/3]**: The number of uses. In this case, using it 3 times depletes the item. (※There are exceptions that can be used infinitely).
* **[15:00] or [2:00:00]**: The waiting time (recast) until it can be used again. Wait 15 minutes or 2 hours, and it will become usable once more.

## 6. Hunting Basics and the Iron Rule of "Re-calling Alter Egos"
Monsters right near the city are weak, and they get stronger the further you go. Start by defeating the nearby ones.

Before you start hunting, **always summon 3 Alter Egos first!**
* **Recommended Setup**: Valaineral (Tank), Tenzen (Attacker), Adelheid (Attacker/Support)
* *※ You can also use Alter Egos obtained from login campaigns. If you have "Shantotto II", available only during specific periods, she is incredibly strong and highly recommended.*

At first, your Alter Egos (especially Valaineral) will defeat enemies in almost one hit, so you'll breeze to around level 10 in just a few minutes.

### ⚠️ The Biggest Trap: "Re-call" your Alter Egos when you level up!
The strength of an Alter Ego depends on the player's level *at the time they were summoned*.
This means that **even if you reach level 10, an Alter Ego summoned when you were level 1 remains at level 1 strength!**

If you feel like you suddenly can't defeat enemies, dismiss your Alter Egos and re-summon them at your current level.
However, the magic used to call them has a recast time (a few minutes of waiting), so **re-calling them roughly every 10 levels** is the perfect pace.

## 7. The First Milestone is Level 20! The Ultimate Shortcut to Jeuno
Your first leveling milestone is **"Level 20"**. Why 20? Because that's when you can accept the quest to ride a "Chocobo (Mount)", which dramatically eases transportation.

Once you hit level 20, return to the city and head to the grand metropolis "Jeuno". In the past, walking there was a perilous journey, but today we have a trick!

1. Talk to the **Unity NPC** in the city.
2. Select **"Wanted encounter area relocation"**.
3. Choose **"Content Level 125"** > **"Batallia Downs"** to warp! (※ Requires 100 Unity points)
4. Upon arriving, **turn around and walk directly behind you**. You are now at the entrance to Upper Jeuno!

---

## 8. Chocobo License on Hold? The Smart Way for Solo Players
Upon entering Upper Jeuno, walk straight ahead, take the stairs down on your left, and head to the "Chocobo Stables". Talk to the old man (Brutus) there to accept the quest **"Wounded Chocobo"**.

* *💡 Caution: The first time you speak to him, a different quest called "Runaway Chocobo" might appear. Keep talking to him until "Wounded Chocobo" triggers.*

For this quest, you nurse a chocobo using an item called "Gausebit Wildgrass". (※ This is now a very friendly feature where you **only have to wait 1 minute** to hand over the next grass, instead of waiting past Earth time midnight!)

### ⚠️ Advice for Solo Players
If a beginner tries to buy "Gausebit Wildgrass" at the Auction House, it's way too expensive. Attempting to farm it yourself is dangerous because the monsters that drop it are a bit too strong for a level 20 character.
Therefore, if you're completely solo, the safest and most recommended approach is to **"just accept the quest for now, and go collect the grass later when you've leveled up and gotten stronger."**

---

## 9. Preparing for a Support Job! Rhapsodies of Vana'diel and "Bee Spollen"
Now that you're level 20, let's unlock FFXI's greatest feature: the **"Support Job (Subjob)"**!
You can accept the quest in either Selbina or Mhaura, but **Selbina** is recommended.

However, doing the regular quest requires tedious item farming, so we'll use a trick route by progressing the **"Rhapsodies of Vana'diel" (RoV)** missions.

### Prep Work: Gather 3 "Bee Spollen"
Before progressing the RoV missions, you can make things drastically easier by preparing **3 "Bee Spollen"** in advance, which will be requested in Chapter 1, Section 3 "Freeing the Shackles".

1. Use the Unity NPC warp to fly to either **Konschtat Highlands** or **La Theine Plateau**.
2. Defeat the bee monsters there (easy wins at level 20) and collect 3 "Bee Spollen".

---

## 10. Once Collected, Head Straight to Selbina!
After gathering the 3 Bee Spollen, travel directly to **"Selbina"** on foot, by chocobo, or by mount (both Konschtat and La Theine connect to the Valkurm Dunes, where Selbina is located).

* **On Foot**: You might get attacked by Goblins or Bats in the dunes, but with your Alter Egos, you can defeat them. Proceed while leveling up along the way.
* **On a Mount**: You will never be attacked by enemies, allowing you to dash through safely.

---

## 11. Support Job Finally Acquired!
Upon arriving in Selbina, progress the RoV mission.
The NPC will ask you to bring 3 Bee Spollen, so triumphantly trade the 3 you just gathered!

This earns you a crucial RoV key item: **"Gilgamesh's introductory letter"**.

With this "key item" in hand, speak to the Support Job NPC (Isacio) in Selbina... and surprisingly, **he will completely skip the item-gathering chore and instantly mark the quest as cleared!**

Congratulations! You have finally unlocked your Support Job!

---

## 12. The Next Goal: Level 30 and Extra Jobs!
Once you acquire your Support Job, speak to the Nomad Moogle in Selbina and **equip any Support Job for now**.
Once set, head outside the city and resume leveling up.

Your next major milestone is **"Level 30"**.
Why aim for 30? Because at level 30, you can finally accept quests to unlock **"Extra Jobs"** (Advanced Jobs), which is one of the greatest joys of FFXI!

---

## 13. The "Level Subjob First" Strategy!
Once you reach level 30 and unlock extra jobs, you'll want to acquire a Support Job that truly fits your playstyle.
For melee/vanguard jobs especially, the starting 6 jobs don't provide great subjob benefits, so using an extra job as your subjob is highly recommended.

* **Warrior**: Sub Samurai, Sub Dragoon, etc.
* **Monk**: Sub Warrior, etc.
* **Thief**: Sub Ninja, etc.

### 💡 Veteran Advice: Level your Subjob to 50 First!
Your Support Job is capped at half the level of your Main Job. (e.g., If you want your Warrior to be 99, your sub Samurai needs to be level 49 or 50).

The easiest and most stress-free way to proceed is to **"level your intended Support Job to 50 first!"**
By capping your subjob at 50 right away, you can then switch back to your main job and take it all the way to 99 without ever worrying about your subjob falling behind.

---

## 14. Why Start with a "Melee Job"?
This guide primarily assumes you are leveling a melee (vanguard) job. You might be wondering, "But I want to play a mage!"
However, I highly recommend starting with a melee job for a few reasons:

1. Mage jobs defeat enemies very slowly early on (your Alter Egos will do all the work).
2. **"Taking a melee job to 99 first makes progressing through various story missions infinitely easier."**

The golden route of modern FFXI is to reach max level on a melee job, use that overwhelming strength to crush story bosses, and then level your desired mage jobs later!

---

## 15. Paralleling Missions for Massive EXP Bonuses!
While leveling, you should also try to progress your **"Starting Nation Missions"** and **"Rhapsodies of Vana'diel (RoV) Missions"** as far as you can.

The biggest reason for this is that progressing RoV missions rewards you with special Key Items ("Rhapsody in...") that **permanently grant massive boosts to your EXP gain!**
Whether you want to play a melee or a mage, grabbing these EXP boosts first will drastically speed up your overall progression.

(※ At first, your level is capped at 50. After that, you'll need to complete "Limit Break" quests to raise the cap by 5 levels at a time. I'll cover those in a separate article. For now, do your best to reach this point!)

---

## 16. What kind of content can completely new players participate in?
I sometimes get asked by brand new players, "Is there any content that new players can participate in?"
To be perfectly honest, there is no system in this game that allows you to jump straight into endgame-like content regardless of your level right from the start.

However, in the past, "Nation Missions" and other storylines required a full 6-person party to clear, so **"progressing through the missions is a major piece of content in itself."** Enjoying the story and completing these missions will be your biggest focus and the primary "content" as a beginner.

Another thing you can enjoy **regardless of your level are the "Seasonal Events."**
These events are designed so that even level 1 characters can participate, and the rewards are often cute "fashion gear" or vanity items.
If you ever feel tired from leveling up or grinding missions, how about taking a break and trying out some of these seasonal events for a change of pace?
