# Gil-Making Guide for Beginners & Intermediate Players

For new adventurers setting foot in Vana'diel or returning veterans coming back after a long hiatus, securing "Gil" is one of the most critical challenges

Depending on your character's level, mission progress, and equipment strength, choosing the **"most efficient and comfortable gil-making method for your current state"** is the key to a comfortable life in Vana'diel!

---

## 🛡️ 1. The Easiest & Most Reliable: Eminence Vendor Method (For Beginners & Fresh Starts)

The easiest and most reliable way to make gil for players starting from scratch is utilizing the **"Eminence Records"** system!

Simply accept and complete Eminence objectives that you can easily clear, earn Eminence Points, exchange them for specific equipment, and sell them to a vendor (NPC) for massive amounts of Gil.

### 🌟 The Only Target to Exchange: "Acheron Shield"!
When exchanging your Eminence Points for equipment, make sure to choose **[Acheron Shield]** without exception

* **Exchange Rate**: 1 Shield = **2,755 Eminence Points**
* **Vendor Sell Price**: 1 Shield = **approx. 27,550 Gil** (Sold to NPC)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Important Notes on Eminence Vendor Method**
> Equipment from Eminence exchanges can generally be sold to vendors at a conversion rate of "1 point = 10 Gil."
> Since the Acheron Shield requires a high amount of points per item, it minimizes the effort (number of times you have to click/mash buttons to exchange) and allows you to sell efficiently. It is by far the most recommended item!
> Note that there is a weekly cap of
> **"100,000 Eminence Points"** for equipment exchanges.
> 
> By selling Acheron Shields up to this limit every week,
> you can reliably earn **approx. 1,000,000 Gil per week**!

## ⚡ 2. Maximize Points! Time-Limited "Gain Experience" Objectives

There is a **"secret time window"** to maximize and exponentially earn both Eminence Points and Unity Points!

Make full use of the **Time-Limited Objective "Gain Experience"**, which is accepted during specific days and hours

### 📅 Active Windows (Japan Standard Time - JST / 3 Times a Week)
* **Every Sunday: 20:00 to 23:59 (JST)** (★Highly Recommended! Most active players are online, making it the easiest time to earn!)
* **Every Tuesday: 12:00 to 15:59 (JST)**
* **Every Thursday: 04:00 to 07:59 (JST)**

During these periods, you will receive massive amounts of Eminence and Unity Points **for every 5,000 Experience Points you earn**! This is an incredibly lucrative bonus time!

> [!TIP]
> **Schedule Your Activities Around Time-Limited Objectives!**
> If you are a beginner or returnee planning to level up or earn Job Points, try to schedule your sessions during these active windows (especially the Sunday night slot)!
> While your level goes up, both your Eminence and Unity Points will pile up at an astonishing speed

---

## 🧪 3. Turn Unity Points into Cash: "Prize Powder" Method!

The accumulated **"Unity Points (UNI)"** you rapidly gathered during time-limited objectives can also be directly converted into a powerful source of Gil!

Once you have gathered Unity Points, talk to any Unity NPC and exchange them for the following item to sell to a vendor

* **Exchange Item**: **[Prize Powder]**
* **Exchange Rate**: 10 Unity Points = 1 Powder
* **Vendor Sell Price**: 1 Powder = **10 Gil** (Sold to NPC)

You might think "Isn't 10 Gil per item too cheap?" However, because Unity Points accumulate in massive quantities, **exchanging 10,000 points into Prize Powder yields 100,000 Gil**, and the cap of **99,999 points yields approx. 1,000,000 Gil**!

This is an extremely excellent supplemental source of quick cash once you have exhausted your weekly 100,000 Eminence exchange limit!

## 💎 4. Steady and Profitable: "Crystal Auction House" Method

Outside of vendor-based methods, the most recommended way for beginners and returnees to make money is **selling "Crystals" on the Auction House**!

* Various crystals obtained when defeating enemies or under the effect of Signet/Sanction/Sigil are consumed in massive quantities daily by crafters for "Synthesis" and guild test items.
* Because of this, transaction volume on the Auction House is extremely high, and if you list them, **they will almost never go unsold and return to your delivery box!**

> [!CAUTION]
> **The Trap of Expensive Equipment and Rare Materials**
> At first glance, selling high-priced equipment or rare materials on the Auction House seems to be more profitable.
> However, these items often suffer from "oversupply causing price crashes," "volatile prices," or "extremely slow sales due to very few buyers." You will often find they don't sell quickly when you need cash immediately.
> 
> On the other hand, the **"Acheron Shield & Prize Powder vendor sales"** and **"Crystal Auction House listings"** will leave you with instant cash in hand. If you need gil immediately, this is absolutely the most efficient and reliable path!👍

## 🌀 5. For Intermediate & Returning Players: Odyssey Chest Opening & Goal Touch

Once you become an intermediate player, advance your missions, and obtain decent equipment, it is time to challenge dedicated battle content to earn even larger sums of Gil!
Your first prime target is the entry-based exploration content **"[Odyssey] (Sheol A, B, C)"** accessed from Rabao

Depending on your progress and playstyle, you can make gil extremely efficiently using the following methods.

### 📦 ① Sheol A & B: Thief Solo "Chest Opening" Survival Guide
In Sheol A and B, a solo strategy called **"Chest Opening"** (opening Chests and Coffers spawned randomly across the map without engaging in combat) is an incredibly lucrative way to earn expensive materials!

* **Dropped Materials**:
  * **Sheol A**: **[Lustrous Scale]** (Sells on the Auction House)
  * **Sheol B**: **[Lustrous Hide]** (Sells on the Auction House)
  * Auction House prices for these materials fluctuate
    depending on server supply.
    Check the transaction prices beforehand and
    choose the area (A or B) with the higher profit margin

#### ⚠️ Essential Rules and Preparation
* **Main Job is "Thief" Only!**: To open chests, you must have Thief's "Pickpocket/Thief's Tools" traits and specific key items.
* **Prepare Key Items**: Use **[Living Key]** or **[Skeleton Key]** to unlock chests.
  * **Living Key** is highly recommended due to its accessibility.
  * While you can buy Living Keys from NPC vendors under certain Conquest conditions, they are quite expensive. Therefore, **crafting them yourself via Bonecraft/Alchemy** is the most cost-effective way! Since the required skill level is not too high, collect materials during your regular adventures, craft them, and enter the area once you have accumulated a good batch

#### 🦖 Defeating the Dreaded "Trap Box (Mimic)" and Survival Tactics!
Chests do not always open successfully. If you hit a "dud," the chest will instantly transform into a powerful **[Mimic]** and attack you!
Since defeating it solo is impossible, you must memorize the following **emergency survival tactics**

> [!CAUTION]
> **Mimic Emergency Escape Manual**
> 1. **Summon Facis Beforehand**: Always ensure you have 2 tank Trusts (such as Valaineral and AAEV) and the rest filled with white mage Trusts for healing and survival.
> 2. **Run Using Trusts as Decoys**: The moment a Mimic appears, let your tank Trusts take its aggression, and immediately run as far away as possible to a safe spot free of other monsters.
> 3. **Log Out Safely in the Safe Zone**: Once you have put enough distance between you and the Mimic, immediately execute **"Log Out"** from the menu while your Trusts hold the line! If successful, you will return safely upon your next login.
> 
> **The Pro Trick When Log Out Fails: "Intentional Death by Non-Mimic Mobs"**
> If your Trusts are wiped out, the Mimic will use a "Draw In" ability, pulling you back to its face no matter how far you run.
> If you realize you cannot log out in time, the best pro trick is to **"use a Reraise item and deliberately let a normal monster defeat you."**
> By dying to a normal mob instead of the Mimic, you won't trigger the Draw In effect, allowing you to safely rise again using Reraise far away from the Mimic!

### 👥 ② Sheol C: Party Goal Touch & Segment Farming
Sheol C has the highest Content Level, and its monsters are extremely powerful, making **solo activity absolutely not recommended**
Furthermore, the material obtained in Sheol C, **[Lustrous Feather]**, is in low demand compared to scales and hides, resulting in flooded auction listings and poor prices.

However, running Sheol C in a **Party** offers massive advantages!

* **Enormous Gil from Goal Touches**: Touching the goal at the end of an Odyssey run rewards you with Gil, which scales with the number of monsters defeated. Sheol C offers the highest base reward. Clearing monsters with a party and touching the goal will yield massive instant Gil!
* **Earn Massive Mog Segments**: To challenge the ultimate bosses in "Odyssey Gaol" later, you will need a huge amount of "Mog Segment" points. Sheol C is by far the most efficient place to farm these points! Whenever you see party recruitment shouts, join actively and accumulate your segments

## 🌌 6. Profitable Daily Routine: "Omen - Stymied (Trash) Route" for Swart Astral Detritus

Another ultimate solo gil-making method for intermediate and returning players is the daily "Stymied (Trash) Route" in the battle content **"[Omen]"** located in Reisenjima!

Accessible once you clear the Rhapsodies of Vana'diel missions, this is one of the best daily routines you can establish

### 🌟 Stack Your Entry Items ("Canteen") for Consecutive Runs!
To enter Omen, you need theだいじなもの "Mystic Canteen" (チカラを閉じ込めた飯盒).

* This canteen can be **stocked up to 3 times at the NPC "Incantrix"**!
* Therefore, by carrying one on hand and claiming the 3 stocked canteens, you can **enter Omen up to 4 times consecutively in a single day**! This allows you to accumulate runs and catch up during weekends

### 💰 Target High-Value "Swart Astral Detritus"!
In the "Stymied (Trash) Route" (where you focus on clearing rooms filled with trash mobs instead of boss progression), enemies frequently drop the valuable material **[Swart Astral Detritus]** (黒の残魂).

* **Swart Astral Detritus**: Required in massive quantities to upgrade RMEA weapons with Oboro's augments. As such, they are always traded at very high prices on the Auction House.
* Clearing Omen once a day (or utilizing your stocked runs) to gather these and selling them is one of the most reliable and steady ways to build up your fortune!

### 🛡️ Recommended Jobs and Tactical Tips
* **"Main Thief" is King!**: Since your goal is item drops, going as main Thief with high Treasure Hunter (TH) yields the best drop rates and maximizes your overall profits.
* **Unconfident? Go "Main Job / Support Thief"**: If your Thief is undergeared and you fear wiping, you can enter with your best, most durable main job and set Thief as your support job (Sapo-shi) to safely secure drops.
* **Accessible but Watch Out**: You do not need to be a Job Master to clear this route, making it highly accessible. However, the number of enemies in the stymied route is extremely large. To avoid getting overwhelmed and wiping, **"pull enemies safely a few at a time to a secure area before defeating them"**👍
