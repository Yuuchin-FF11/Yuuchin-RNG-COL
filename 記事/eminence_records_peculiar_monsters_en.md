# "Defeat Peculiar Monsters!" All 15 Targets Optimized Farming Route Guide

This guide describes the most efficient route to defeat all 15 target NMs for the Eminence Records Quest 4 objective "Defeat Peculiar Monsters!" in a single run.

---

## Preparation
* Set your Home Point (HP) in East Adoulin near the Mog House (M).
* Prepare a way to warp/teleport back (Dejon Ring, Dejon Dagger, Warp Scrolls, etc.).

---

## Efficient Farming Route (15 Steps)

### [Phase 1: East Adoulin HP Base]
Start from your registered East Adoulin HP and cover the Adoulin and nearby outdoor zones.

1. Labyrinth of Onzozo (Correction: Yahse/Lala Waterways) -> Lala Waterways (Eminence Obj #13)
   * Target NM: Awoken Hakenmann (Orobon)
   * Move: Area change directly from East Adoulin into Lala Waterways.
   * Return: After defeat, use Warp/Dejon to return to East Adoulin HP.

2. Kamihr Drifts (Eminence Obj #12)
   * Target NM: Awoken Nihhus (Wivre)
   * Move: Use the Waypoint in East Adoulin to warp to Outer Ra'Kaznar (via Mystical Silts), then exit to Kamihr Drifts.
   * Return: After defeat, use Warp/Dejon to return to East Adoulin HP.

3. Xarcabard (Eminence Obj #15)
   * Target NM: Awoken Hrani (Demon)
   * Move: Use the Survival Guide (Book) in front of the Mog House to warp to Castle Zvahl Baileys, then exit to Xarcabard.

4. Arrapago Reef (Eminence Obj #7)
   * Target NM: Awoken Morbol Emperor (Morbol)
   * Move: Return to Castle Zvahl, use the Survival Guide to warp to Caedarva Mire (or nearby), then move to Arrapago Reef.
   * Return: After defeat, use Warp/Dejon to return to Adoulin.

---

### [Phase 2: West Adoulin HP & Unity Warp Run]
Change your HP to West Adoulin (e.g., in front of the Mog House) and make full use of Unity Warps.

5. Western Altepa Desert (Eminence Obj #9)
   * Target NM: Awoken Dendainsonne (Behemoth)
   * Move: Use Unity Warp (CL125).

6. Caedarva Mire (Eminence Obj #3)
   * Target NM: Awoken Vampyr Jarl (Vampyr)
   * Move: Use Unity Warp (CL135) to Hediva Island.

7. Wajaom Woodlands (Eminence Obj #1)
   * Target NM: Awoken Hildesvini (Marid)
   * Move: Use Unity Warp (CL135 or CL125).

8. Aydeewa Subterrane (Eminence Obj #6)
   * Target NM: Awoken Hrungnir (Golem)
   * Move: Use Unity Warp (CL145) or Survival Guide.

9. Beaucedine Glacier (Eminence Obj #4)
   * Target NM: Awoken Gorgimera (Khimaira)
   * Move: Use Unity Warp to "Fei'Yin (CL128)" and exit immediately to Beaucedine Glacier.

---

### [Phase 3: Battalia to Mount Zhayolm & Reisenjima]
Connect from Jeuno areas to Aht Urhgan and Reisenjima using unique teleport networks.

10. Battalia Downs (Eminence Obj #10)
    * Target NM: Awoken Freke (Cerberus)
    * Move: Use Unity Warp (CL122) to Battalia Downs.
    * Note: Do NOT use Warp/Dejon after defeating the NM. Head back towards Jeuno on foot.

11. Mount Zhayolm (Eminence Obj #2)
    * Target NM: Awoken Mokkuralfi (Flan)
    * Move: Speak to the Voidwatch (VW) Warp NPC near Jeuno and choose "Aht Urhgan Route" -> "Mount Zhayolm".

12. Reisenjima (Eminence Obj #8)
    * Target NM: Awoken Stoorworm (Hydra)
    * Move: After defeating the Mount Zhayolm NM, use a Teleport Ring (or Rhapsody Key Item Warp) to Reisinjima and travel to Ingress #9.
    * Return: After defeat, use Warp/Dejon to return to town.

---

### [Phase 4: Final HP Warp Sprint]
Finish the run using Home Point (HP) Warps for the final 3 zones.

13. Movalpolos Waterways (Eminence Obj #14)
    * Target NM: Awoken Andhrimnir (Course)
    * Move: Use Home Point Warp to Movalpolos Waterways.

14. Palborough Mines (Eminence Obj #5)
    * Target NM: Awoken Ariri Samariri (Poroggo)
    * Move: Use Home Point Warp to Palborough Mines.

15. Qufim Island (Eminence Obj #11)
    * Target NM: Awoken Tanngrisnir (Dragon/Dahak)
    * Move: Use Home Point Warp to Qufim Island.

---

## After Completion
Once the 15th NM in Qufim Island is defeated, use HP Warp to Port Jeuno, talk to the NPC Elijah to report, and claim your rewards (A.M.A.N. Cooper / Silver Voucher and Corrosive Psyche).
