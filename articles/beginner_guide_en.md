# Beginner & Returning Player's Guide

Welcome to Vana'diel! This guide is for those who are starting FFXI for the first time or returning after a long hiatus.

## First Steps
1. **Records of Eminence**: This is your primary way to earn experience and gear.
2. **Trusts**: Summon NPCs to help you in battle.
3. **Home Points & Survival Guides**: Essential for fast travel.

## Progression
* Focus on completing the main story missions (Rhapsodies of Vana'diel).
* Join a Linkshell to get help from veterans.
