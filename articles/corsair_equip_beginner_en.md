# Corsair Equipment Guide (Budget / Entry-level)

This page introduces **"Budget Gear (Entry-level)"** sets that are relatively easy to collect for those who have just started Corsair or cannot yet prepare high-end equipment such as Nyame or Ikenga sets.
Please use this as a reference for gathering equipment first and gradually stepping up to **[High-end Equipment (Ideal Sets)](article_en.html?file=articles/corsair_equip_en.md)**.

> **⚠️ Prerequisite: Collect the "Malignance Set" first!**
> This "Budget Gear Guide" assumes that you have already collected the **"Malignance Armor Set"**, which can be obtained from the High-Tier Mission Battlefield "★The Lady of Wings."
> For Corsair, Malignance Armor has exceptional performance with high levels of Accuracy, Store TP, Damage Taken Cut, and Magic Evasion. In current FFXI, it is no exaggeration to say that it is essential equipment (must-have gear) whether you are fighting in melee or performing ranged attacks.
> If you don't have it yet, start by farming "★The Lady of Wings" (difficulty: Very Easy to Normal, or any difficulty you can repeat) to complete all five pieces of the Malignance set!

### Equipment Points & Commentary
* **Combination of Main Weapon and Neck (Regal Necklace)**
  If you have just started Corsair and do not have "Regal Necklace" yet, your top priority should be equipping "Lanun Knife (Path C)," which has "Phantom Roll +7," to boost the basic performance of your rolls.
  Once you obtain the "Regal Necklace," you can secure "Phantom Roll +7" with the neck slot alone. In this state (unless you have the purpose of further extending the roll duration), there is no problem with the roll performance even if you change the main weapon to "Lanun Knife (Path A)" or "Lanun Knife (Path B)."
  However, please note that you will no longer receive the benefit of the "Phantom Roll: HP/MP Recovery" augment of Lanun Knife (Path C), so use them according to the party's situation.

* **Damage Taken Cut (Durability) via Dual Wielding Main/Sub**
  The basic property of Rostam or Lanun Knife, "Damage Taken -X% per Phantom Roll," is **properly applied even when held in the sub-weapon slot**.
  If you are not confident in your damage cut equipment (e.g., you don't have a Defending Ring yet), it is recommended to equip Lanun Knives in both main and sub slots (Dual Wield). If two rolls are active, you can secure an excellent cut of "Damage Taken -16%" with weapons alone, significantly increasing your survival rate.

* **Reasons for Fixing Sub and Ranged Weapons (Sortie Compatibility and Compensator Handling)**
  The reason for fixing the sub weapon to "Kustawi +1 (Rank 15)" and the ranged weapon to "Fomalhaut" is primarily to prepare for maneuvering in the endgame content "Sortie," which you will challenge in the future.
  Corsairs in Sortie have the role of following up on the Scholar's skillchains with their own weapon skills. If you change weapons during gear swapping, your accumulated TP will vanish, so it's necessary to fix the weapon slots to maintain TP.
  The "Compensator," famous as roll gear, has "Phantom Roll Duration +20," but swapping to this every time you roll resets your hard-earned TP to 0. In Sortie, since "Bolter's Roll" is frequently reapplied for movement, losing important TP for a 20-second extension is too great a disadvantage.
  This doesn't mean you "must not use it," and if you have no better weapons, adopting the Compensator is fine. However, in that case, be careful to **"stay equipped with the Compensator even when shooting Last Stand (do not swap)"** to prevent TP loss.
  Furthermore, as an important note, if you shoot Last Stand with a weapon other than "Fomalhaut," you can follow up on a Scholar's "Fragmentation" skillchain to create "Light," but you cannot shoot again to generate the ultimate skillchain "Radiance" (Radiance is a privilege of Fomalhaut).
  From these perspectives of "TP Maintenance" and "Ultimate Skillchains," we recommend shifting to a fixed combination of "Kustawi +1 (Rank 15)" and "Fomalhaut" once you reach the stage where you are conscious of skillchains.
  By the way, even if you don't have Fomalhaut yet, it is fully possible to obtain triggers for Sortie Sector B solo using "Kunishige" or "Ataktos" with this budget gear.

* **Balancing Damage Taken Cut, Enmity Minus, and Roll Duration**
  For armor and accessories, the combination ensures that duration and range properties for Phantom Rolls are secured while maintaining the "Damage Taken Cut Cap (-50% or more)" even while using rolls.
  Also, since Phantom Rolls can surprisingly generate Enmity (hate), we've consciously incorporated "Enmity Minus" into the equipment to reduce the risk of taking the enemy's target.

* **Reason for Adopting Carrier's Sash (Waist)**
  In FFXI, having even +1 in elemental resistance increases the resist rate against enemy debuffs and special attacks. Therefore, we've adopted the Carrier's Sash, which raises all elemental resistances, to enhance stability.

### Equipment Points & Commentary

* **Balance of Store TP and R.Acc (Adjustment Unique to Corsair)**
  Corsair has slightly lower base R.Acc (Ranged Accuracy) compared to Hunter. Therefore, while emphasizing "Store TP (STP)" to accumulate TP quickly, since attacks are meaningless if they don't hit, the combination focuses on **"securing a slightly thicker amount of R.Acc"** than Hunter gear configurations.

* **Regarding Obtaining Head Gear (Ishkur Banner)**
  "Ishkur Banner," a candidate for the head slot, can be easily obtained by participating in the Aht Urhgan content "Besieged" and exchanging 5,000 Imperial Standing points. If you see Besieged occurring, participate actively and save up points!

* **Malignance Armor Set is Super Excellent for Defense!**
  For ranged impact (normal shooting to accumulate TP), it is highly recommended to use the "Malignance Armor Set" for all five slots for the time being. Malignance Armor is truly excellent gear, securing the R.Acc that Corsair tends to lack while also significantly increasing the "Store TP" needed to accumulate TP quickly.

* **How to Choose Accessory Slots (R.Acc and STP Boost)**
  Once you have solidified your armor with the Malignance set, focus on selecting equipment that can further boost "Store TP" or "R.Acc" for the remaining slots (Neck, Ear, Ring, Waist, Back, etc.) to achieve a well-balanced setup. Since Corsair has slightly lower base R.Acc compared to Hunter, adjusting accessory slots slightly toward R.Acc will provide stability when facing tough enemies where you are unsure if attacks will hit.

### Equipment Points & Commentary

* **Purpose is "Accumulating TP Instantly" rather than "Damage"**
  This set is separate from the standard "Quick Draw gear" intended for damage or debuffing. It is specialized gear purely for the purpose of **"instantly gaining TP by firing Quick Draw."** Since Quick Draw is a guaranteed hit and Store TP applies fully, you can gain a large amount of TP in a single shot by equipping STP-heavy gear.

* **Essential Technique for Follow-up Skillchains (Magic Burst) in Sortie**
  This equipment shines most in endgame "Sortie" combat.
  In Sortie, a strategy where a Corsair immediately follows up on a Scholar's skillchain with a WS (Weapon Skill) to further extend the chain for Magic Burst (MB) damage is extremely important.
  To avoid being late to the Scholar's skillchain, this "Quick Draw STP Set" plays an indispensable role as a means of instantly securing TP for the follow-up.

* **Since it's a guaranteed hit, you only need to prioritize "STP" (Easy substitutions)**
  While equipment examples are listed above, some, like the "Yemaya Belt" for the waist, might be a bit tough to prepare in the early stages.
  But rest assured. Quick Draw is a "guaranteed hit," and since this set is not for damage, there's no need to worry excessively about Magic Accuracy or Magic Attack. Anything that raises "Store TP (STP)" is fine.
  For example, for the waist slot, try flexibly combining with STP gear you have on hand, such as substituting with "Kentarch Belt +1" obtained from Unity Wanted.

  **【Example 1: Aiming for Light Route (Radiance)】**
  When a Scholar creates a "Fragmentation" skillchain, a Corsair firing "Last Stand" with Fomalhaut can connect it to a "Light" skillchain.
  Furthermore, shooting Last Stand with Fomalhaut grants a unique AM (Aftermath). By firing two Quick Draws immediately after that Light skillchain to gain TP and then firing "Last Stand" again, you can generate the ultimate skillchain "Radiance," which promises significant damage.

  **【Example 2: Aiming for Dark Route (Consecutive Darkness)】**
  When a Scholar creates a "Distortion" skillchain, a Corsair following up with "Leaden Salute" results in a "Darkness" skillchain.
  Immediately after, fire two Quick Draws to quickly regain TP and fire "Wildfire" to generate another "Darkness" skillchain.

  **【Example 3: Aiming for Gravitation Route (Skillchain with Rune Fencer)】**
  Bosses in Sortie Sector B and F have modes where they shroud themselves in Lightning or Wind attributes. In Lightning mode, the basic strategy is for the Scholar to create a "Gravitation" skillchain for Earth MB, but a Corsair alone cannot connect "Gravitation" to a "Darkness" skillchain.
  However, if a Rune Fencer tank fires a WS to connect to a "Darkness" skillchain, a Corsair can immediately fire "Wildfire" after that Darkness chain to generate another "Darkness" skillchain. This isn't a frequent situation, but it's a useful technique to remember for boosting damage.

  To establish such consecutive WS in a short time, this gear set for immediate TP recovery through Quick Draw shines.

  **【Supplement 1: TP Assistance from Tactician's Roll】**
  By rolling "Tactician's Roll" (Regain effect) in advance before a boss battle, you can start combat with TP already accumulated.
  Since TP continues to accumulate gradually even after firing a WS or during Quick Draw, if the boss cannot be defeated in one skillchain and the Scholar creates another, it becomes easier to secure TP in time, allowing for stable consecutive follow-ups.

  **【Supplement 2: Support Jobs (Ninja vs. Samurai)】**
  The configuration in this article is basically written based on "Support Job: Ninja (Sapo Nin)."
  If you want to make participation in skillchains easier, changing to "Sapo Samurai" is highly recommended. With Sapo Samurai, you have "Meditate" and "Sekkanoki," making TP acquisition and consecutive WS much easier.
  *By the way, the reason the author favors Sapo Nin is that in situations where Invis and Sneak are needed during Sortie movement, it's overwhelmingly easier and more comfortable to reapply them with Ninjutsu (Tonko/Monomi) than consuming powders and oils. Choose your support job according to your own playstyle and preferences!*

### Equipment Points & Commentary
* **Prioritize "Hitting Guaranteed" over Damage!**
  In the early stages where equipment is not yet gathered, I think it is quite a hurdle to prepare equipment that increases Weapon Skill Damage (WSD) for each slot. If there is a slot where WSD can be increased with what you have on hand, adopt it; if it is difficult, you don't have to try to stack WSD by force.

### Equipment Points & Commentary (Wildfire, Hot Shot, etc.)
* **Start by Firing with "Magic Attack" Focused Gear!**
  WF (Wildfire) is a Fire-element Magic Weapon Skill, and Hot Shot is a physical/Fire hybrid WS. Ideally, balancing "Magic Attack" and "Weapon Skill Damage (WSD)" is best, but gathering strong WSD gear in the early stages is tough.
  Therefore, at this stage, don't worry too much about difficult balancing; just gathering and firing with whatever "Magic Attack" gear you have on hand will result in a visible damage increase. Let's start by dealing massive damage with a Magic Attack specialized configuration.

* **Precautions for "R.Acc" when using Hot Shot**
  Since Hot Shot is not pure magic but also includes a "physical attribute" as a hybrid WS, damage will not result if the initial physical accuracy check misses. Therefore, depending on the enemy or situation you are shooting Hot Shot at, pay attention to "hitting guaranteed" by firmly stacking not only Magic Attack but also "R.Acc (Ranged Accuracy)."

* **Effects of Various "Elemental Obis (Hachirin-no-Obi, etc.)"**
  Elemental belts like the "Hachirin-no-Obi" (Dark) in the Leaden Salute gear or "Hachirin-no-Obi" (Fire) in the WF gear are designed to exert a powerful damage-increasing effect only when the current "Day" or "Weather" matches that attribute.
  If the day or weather does not match, there is no effect. So if there is a Scholar in the party, ask for "Weather Magic (Storm)" corresponding to the attribute of the WS you want to fire.

### Equipment Points & Commentary
* **Securing "Dual Wield +11" and Utilizing Malignance Armor**
  When performing melee combat as Sapo Nin, you need to secure "Dual Wield +11" in gear properties to reach the minimum attack interval (cap).
  How to earn this value is a troubling point for Corsair, but in this configuration, "Dual Wield +6" from "Adhemar Jacket +1 (Path A)" and "Dual Wield +5" from "Suppanomimi" are combined to achieve exactly "+11" without waste.
  Then, for the remaining armor slots (Head, Hands, Legs, Feet) after securing Dual Wield, "Malignance Armor" is packed in for all of them. By centering on Malignance Armor, the greatest strength is that you can simultaneously secure "Accuracy" required as a melee frontliner, "Damage Taken Cut" to withstand enemy attacks, and "Store TP" to accelerate TP accumulation at a high level.

* **Precautions during Operation (Durability)**
  This equipment configuration has many hits and excellent TP efficiency, and high "Magic Evasion" is also secured thanks to Malignance. Regarding Damage Taken Cut, it is also assembled to keep a value close to the cap of -50%, which assumes the benefit of two rolls (-8%) from the property of the main weapon "Lanun Knife" (Damage Taken -4% per Phantom Roll). It is sufficiently tough even without rolls, but by always maintaining roll status, it will be able to exert its full durability.

* **Calculation of Damage Taken Cut (Important: Why Aim for 51% or More)**
  In FFXI's system specifications, the cap for damage cut via equipment is said to be "-50%." However, in the game's internal calculations, fractional processing occurs, so even if you match the notation value on equipment to exactly -50%, it often doesn't actually reach the cap internally.
  Therefore, beginners should definitely remember that "the cap is -50%, but when assembling equipment, aim for slightly more, such as -51% to -53%, just in case."
  In this configuration as well, physical cut is carefully considered by adding the mantle's "Physical Damage Taken -10%" for a total of -59% (reaching the practical cap of -50%).

### Equipment Points & Commentary
* **Prioritize "STR" and "MND" without overdoing it at first!**
  Savage Blade is a WS whose damage depends heavily on "STR" and "MND" stats. The ideal is to stack plenty of Weapon Skill Damage (WSD), but gathering WSD gear in the early stages is tough. If you have equipment that increases WSD on hand, incorporate it; for slots where preparation is difficult, you can deal sufficient damage just by choosing gear that significantly extends "STR" and "MND." Let's try to be creative with the equipment you have without overdoing it.

* **Regarding Dedicated Gear (Laksamana's Frac, Lanun Boots)**
  The body piece "Laksamana's Frac" and feet "Lanun Boots" may feel difficult to prepare in the early stages because they cost a bit of money or effort to enhance. However, they are by no means unreachable, and once they are gathered, the firepower of Savage Blade will grow dramatically, so it's highly recommended to work hard and collect them little by little as future goals.

* **Always make Fotia Gorget and Fotia Belt!**
  "Fotia Gorget" for the neck and "Fotia Belt" for the waist are ultra-excellent pieces that powerfully boost the firepower of Savage Blade. Despite having top-class performance, they are relatively easy to make even for beginners in the early stages by collecting materials (Elemental Gorgets and Elemental Belts), so please make these two with priority.

### Equipment Points & Commentary
* **Ironclad Survivability via Full Malignance**
  Use this set when challenging slightly higher-level content where enemy magic or special attacks hit too hard with your usual gear. By wearing the full Malignance set, you can secure the "Damage Taken Cut Cap (-50%)" and top-class "Magic Evasion," balancing overwhelming survivability with TP rotation rate.

* **Securing "Dual Wield +11" when in Full Malignance**
  When going full Malignance, the question arises: "How do I secure Dual Wield +11?"
  Normally, the standard is to equip "Reiki Yotai (Waist: Dual Wield +7)" and "Eabani Earring (Ear: Dual Wield +4)" to secure +11. However, in the early stages, obtaining these (especially Reiki Yotai) is quite difficult.
  Therefore, as a realistic solution that is relatively easy for beginners to collect, the following two methods are recommended:
  ① Combine **"Shetal Stone (Waist: +6)" + "Suppanomimi (Ear: +5)"** to achieve exactly +11.
  ② Add **"Dual Wield +10" via augment** to the back gear "Camulus's Mantle" obtained in Ambuscade to compensate all at once.
  Let's use these to secure Dual Wield effectively according to your gear on hand and difficulty of acquisition.

* **Beware of "Over-stacking" Dual Wield!**
  Dual Wield values reach the attack interval cap (shortest) at "+11," and equipping more than this will not make attacks any faster. On the contrary, there is a terrifying trap in FFXI's system: "If you stack too much Dual Wield, the amount of TP gained per hit (TP accumulation) will decrease."
  As a result, TP efficiency will visibly deteriorate, so it's a rule to assemble without ever exceeding "+11" for Dual Wield. Please remember that "even a slightly insufficient state (+10, etc.) is better than over-stacking."
