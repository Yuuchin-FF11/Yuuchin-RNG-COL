# Ranger Equipment Guide (High-End & Ideal)

This page introduces **"High-End (Ideal) Equipment Sets for Endgame Content"** for players who want to master Ranger.
Since Ranger cannot truly shine in ranged accuracy (R.Acc) and firepower with halfway gear, we do not provide "Entry-level (Uniqlo)" gear sets here. Instead, all setups are presented with the highest tier of gear as the final goal.

> **💡 Ranger's Gear Philosophy**
> As ranged combat specialists, Rangers have inherently high base R.Acc (Ranged Accuracy) compared to Corsairs.
> Therefore, when configuring gear, it is crucial to focus on "boosting Store TP (STP) and Double Attack (DA) to increase attacks and TP efficiency" rather than over-stacking R.Acc.
> In addition, to not only attack safely from behind tanks in high-difficulty content but also to withstand powerful enemy area-of-effect abilities, it is essential for a top-tier Ranger to be conscious of "Damage Taken Cut (DT)" and "Magic Evasion (M.Eva)" even during gear swaps.
