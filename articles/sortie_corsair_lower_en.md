# Sortie Corsair Strategy Guide (Lower Sectors: GEFH)

## Basics of Lower Sector Strategy

In the lower sectors, the general approach is to head straight to the boss room using Sneak and Invisible. However, in some cases, you may defeat the Naraka NM in Sector G. The bosses are basically empowered versions of the upper sector bosses, so the elemental weaknesses and skillchain patterns follow the same rules.

> **Note on GEO's Indi-Spells**
> In boss battles, the Geomancer basically uses **"Indi-Acumen"** to boost the party's magic power. However, depending on the setup, they may choose **"Indi-Haste."** This is used not so much for attack speed but to **speed up the recast of spells like elemental magic to increase rotation rate.** It's key to have them switch flexibly according to the situation.

### Roll Management: Focusing only on Wizard's Roll

In lower sector boss battles, it is generally recommended for the Corsair to use **only "Wizard's Roll."**

*   **Why omit Monk's Roll?**
    Quick movement after defeating a boss is crucial in the lower sectors. To prioritize recast management, it's efficient to fight only with <span class="ability-highlight">Wizard's Roll</span> and avoid using <span class="ability-highlight">Monk's Roll</span> unless necessary.
    *(Reason: If you use Monk's Roll before combat starts, the recast might not be recovered when you try to use **"Bolter's Roll"** for movement immediately after the kill, causing a delay.)*
*   **【Exception】 Monk's Roll is Mandatory for H Boss (Aita)**
    However, **for the H boss battle only, you MUST use "Monk's Roll."** "**Subtle Blow** (reducing the enemy's TP gain)" is extremely important to prevent the boss from using special moves. Survival and gimmick stability take priority over recast management here.

### Skillchain Follow-up (Exenterator) and Securing Accuracy

Since lower sector bosses have **extremely high evasion**, you must pay close attention to your equipment and food.

*   **Switch to Accuracy-Focused Gear**
    When firing <span class="ws-highlight">Exenterator</span>, switch to an equipment set that maximizes "Melee Accuracy" and stand by. If you miss and break the skillchain, the party's total damage will drop significantly. Prioritize "ensuring it hits" over damage.
    *(See [Exenterator Accuracy Gear here](article_en.html?file=articles/corsair_equip_en.md#exzen-acc))*
*   **Recommended Food: "Sublime Sushi"**
    Choose "Sublime Sushi." This is not only for boosting melee accuracy but also because there are situations in the **H boss battle where you use "<span class="ws-highlight">Last Stand</span>."** By eating "Sublime Sushi," which provides the highest boost to both physical and ranged accuracy, you can stabilize both melee and ranged follow-ups.

## Sector G Boss Guide: Triboulex

The Sector G boss is an empowered version of the upper C boss (Skomora). Like the C boss, it is weak to fire, so the strategy revolves around **Fire Magic Bursts (MB)**.

### Regarding the Naraka NM (Gyvewrapped Naraka) on the way
When defeating the Naraka NM on the way to the G boss, there are two pulling patterns:

*   **Safe Pull**: Pull the Naraka and drag it to a nearby safe hallway to defeat it.
*   **"Aggro-Pull" to the Boss Room**: Cancel Invisible and intentionally let the Naraka aggro you, then run all the way to the boss room while dragging it. Use **Bolter's Roll** to keep distance and re-apply Invisible (or have an ally cast it on you) once safe.

#### Rolls and Support for the Naraka
The Naraka is quickly defeated with Fire MBs. Since there's time during this fight, apply **"Monk's Roll"** here. Upon arriving at the boss room after killing the NM, you can overwrite with "Wizard's Roll" to start the boss battle in an ideal state.

### G Boss Combat
*   **Maintain Debuffs**: It's important for the BLM or SCH to land **"Impact"** and for the BLM to maintain **"Burn"** to lower the enemy's INT.
*   **Skillchain Extension via Exenterator**: The COR should follow up the SCH's "Fusion" skillchain with **"<span class="ws-highlight">Exenterator</span>"** to extend it to a Light SC, boosting the MB window and total damage.

## Sector E Boss Guide: Dhartok

The Sector E boss is an empowered version of the upper A boss (Ghatjot). The basic elemental and skillchain patterns are the same, but since its HP is significantly increased, focus on maximizing damage. The flow of matching <span class="ws-highlight">Exenterator</span> to the SCH's Fusion and supporting with **Fire Shot** is the same as the upper sectors.

## Sector F Boss Guide: Gartell

The Sector F boss is an empowered version of the upper B boss (Leshonn). Its attribute will be either **Thunder (Earth weakness)** or **Wind (Ice weakness)**.

*   **Resource Conservation and Attribute Adaptation**:
    If the boss is weak to Earth, the Scholar creates Gravitation. Since the Corsair has few ways to follow up solo, stick to following up with **"<span class="ws-highlight">Wildfire</span>"** only if the Rune Fencer connects it, and conserve resources.
    The moment the attribute changes to Ice weakness (Wind attribute), fully utilize your stored TP and shots to burst it down with the **Leaden Salute → 2 Shots → Wildfire** skillchain extension.
    > **[How to create/use this solo SC macro (See Upper Sectors Guide)](article_en.html?file=articles/sortie_corsair_en.md#timing-of-skillchain-follow-up-ws)**

## Sector H Boss Guide: Aita

The Sector H boss is an empowered version of the upper D boss (Degei) and, as its appearance suggests, inherits the **"Hades" gimmicks**.

### Aita Special Moves & Weakness Attribute Table

| Boss Special Move | Boss Element | **Weakness Element** | Skillchain Type | **WS for Follow-up** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flaming Kick** | Fire | Water | Distortion | **<span class="ws-highlight">Leaden Salute</span>** |
| **Flash Flood** | Water | Thunder | Fragmentation | **<span class="ws-highlight">Last Stand</span>** |
| **Fulminous Smash** | Thunder | Earth | Gravitation | **<span class="ws-highlight">Wildfire</span>** (※1) |
| **Eroding Flesh** | Earth | Wind | Fragmentation | **<span class="ws-highlight">Last Stand</span>** |
| **Icy Grasp** | Ice | Fire | Fusion | **<span class="ws-highlight">Exenterator</span>** |

(※1) You cannot connect solo against a Gravitation SC, but if the Tank or another ally connects it, you can follow up with Wildfire as a Darkness SC.

> **[See the Radiance (Fragmentation follow-up) macro explanation here (Upper Sectors Guide)](article_en.html?file=articles/sortie_corsair_en.md#laststand-macro)**

### Key Points for Aita
*   **"Vivisection" Reset**: The boss uses the wide-area special move "Vivisection" roughly every 3 minutes, which resets the current weakness. Watch for the next special move to identify the new weakness.
*   **Corsair's Actions**:
    *   **Maintain <span class="ability-highlight">Monk's Roll</span>**: Prioritize lowering the boss's TP gain to reduce the frequency of special moves.
    *   **<span class="ability-highlight">Random Deal</span>**: Use immediately after combat starts to recover the Scholar's "Stratagem" charges.
    *   **<span class="sp-highlight">Wild Card</span>**: The best timing is immediately after the Rune Fencer lands "Rayke + Gambit."
