# Ultimate Gil-Making Guide for Beginners & Returnees

For new adventurers setting foot in Vana'diel or returning veterans coming back after a long hiatus, securing "Gil" is one of the most critical challenges🐾💖

While your accessible content and areas might be limited right after starting or returning, here is the **"easiest and fastest ultimate gil-making method"** that anyone can start from absolute zero!

---

## 🛡️ 1. The Easiest & Most Reliable: Eminence Vendor Method

The easiest and most reliable way to make gil for players starting from scratch is utilizing the **"Eminence Records"** system!

Simply accept and complete Eminence objectives that you can easily clear, earn Eminence Points, exchange them for specific equipment, and sell them to a vendor (NPC) for massive amounts of Gil.

### 🌟 The Only Target to Exchange: "Acheron Shield"!
When exchanging your Eminence Points for equipment, make sure to choose **[Acheron Shield]** without exception🐾

* **Exchange Rate**: 1 Shield = **2,755 Eminence Points**
* **Vendor Sell Price**: 1 Shield = **approx. 27,550 Gil** (Sold to NPC)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Important Notes on Eminence Vendor Method**
> Equipment from Eminence exchanges can generally be sold to vendors at a conversion rate of "1 point = 10 Gil."
> Since the Acheron Shield requires a high amount of points per item, it minimizes the effort (number of times you have to click/mash buttons to exchange) and allows you to sell efficiently. It is by far the most recommended item!
> ※Note that there is a weekly cap of **"100,000 Eminence Points"** for equipment exchanges. By selling Acheron Shields up to this limit every week, you can reliably earn **approx. 1,000,000 Gil per week**!

---

## ⚡ 2. Maximize Points! Sunday Night "Gain Experience" Objective

There is a **"secret time window"** to maximize and exponentially earn both Eminence Points and Unity Points!

Make full use of the **Time-Limited Objective "Gain Experience"**, which is accepted during specific days and hours🐾💖

### 📅 Active Windows (Japan Standard Time - JST / 3 Times a Week)
* **Every Sunday: 20:00 to 23:59 (JST)** (★Highly Recommended! Most active players are online, making it the easiest time to earn!)
* **Every Tuesday: 12:00 to 15:59 (JST)**
* **Every Thursday: 04:00 to 07:59 (JST)**

During this period, you will receive massive amounts of Eminence and Unity Points **for every 5,000 Experience Points you earn**! This is an incredibly lucrative bonus time!

> [!TIP]
> **Schedule Your Activities Around Time-Limited Objectives!**
> If you are a beginner or returnee planning to level up or earn Job Points, try to schedule your sessions during these active windows (especially the Sunday night slot)!
> While your level goes up, both your Eminence and Unity Points will pile up at an astonishing speed🐾

---

## 🧪 3. Turn Unity Points into Cash: "Prize Powder" Method!

The **"Unity Points (UNI)"** you accumulated rapidly during Sunday night's time-limited objectives can also be directly converted into a powerful source of Gil!

Once you have gathered Unity Points, talk to any Unity NPC and exchange them for the following item to sell to a vendor🐾

* **Exchange Item**: **[Prize Powder]**
* **Exchange Rate**: 10 Unity Points = 1 Powder
* **Vendor Sell Price**: 1 Powder = **10 Gil** (Sold to NPC)

You might think "Isn't 10 Gil per item too cheap?" However, because Unity Points accumulate in massive quantities, **exchanging 10,000 points into Prize Powder yields 100,000 Gil**, and the cap of **99,999 points yields approx. 1,000,000 Gil**!

This is an extremely excellent supplemental source of quick cash once you have exhausted your weekly 100,000 Eminence exchange limit!💖

---

## 💎 4. Steady and Profitable: "Crystal Auction House" Method

Outside of vendor-based methods, the most recommended way for beginners and returnees to make money is **selling "Crystals" on the Auction House**!

* Various crystals obtained when defeating enemies or under the effect of Signet/Sanction/Sigil are consumed in massive quantities daily by crafters for "Synthesis" and guild test items.
* Because of this, transaction volume on the Auction House is extremely high, and if you list them, **they will almost never go unsold and return to your delivery box!**

> [!CAUTION]
> **The Trap of Expensive Equipment and Rare Materials**
> At first glance, selling high-priced equipment or rare materials on the Auction House seems to be more profitable.
> However, these items often suffer from "oversupply causing price crashes," "volatile prices," or "extremely slow sales due to very few buyers." You will often find they don't sell quickly when you need cash immediately.
> 
> On the other hand, the **"Acheron Shield & Prize Powder vendor sales"** and **"Crystal Auction House listings"** will leave you with instant cash in hand. If you need gil immediately, this is absolutely the most efficient and reliable path!👍🐾
