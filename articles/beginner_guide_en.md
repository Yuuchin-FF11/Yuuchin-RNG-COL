# Priority Goals for New & Returning Players

To all the adventurers who have newly landed in Vana'diel and those returning after a long hiatus:
This guide explains where to start and what to prioritize in order to challenge today's endgame content (Sortie, Odyssey Gaol, etc.).

## 1. Aim for Level 99 and IL (Item Level) Gear First

In today's FFXI, the first major milestone is **reaching Level 99 and obtaining "IL (Item Level) Equipment."**
Once you have a full set of IL119 gear, you will be able to progress through most missions (storylines) solo and clear simple content.

### Modern Vana'diel is All About "Warping"! Ultra-Comfortable Travel Systems

One of the biggest surprises for returning players from the old days, as well as complete newcomers, is modern Vana'diel's **warp speed travel systems**!

You no longer have to spend hours running for your life just to get to Jeuno (lol). Thanks to the two warp features below, travel has become incredibly smooth:

*   **Home Point (HP) Warp**
    *   These are the crystal structures (Home Points) located in towns and key areas. Once you touch and activate one, you can warp instantly from any other Home Point.
*   **Survival Guide Warp**
    *   These are the floating "books" found in fields and dungeon entrances. Once touched, you can warp instantly between any activated books.

#### Ironclad Rule: "Always Touch" Them When You See Them!
When you arrive in a new area, town, or field, **never forget to touch all the Home Points and Survival Guides in the vicinity first!**

It's easy to think "I'll do it later" at first, but having these warps unlocked makes a 100x difference in efficiency when you're progressing through missions (story) or leveling up.

**"Traveling leisurely on foot or by Chocobo while enjoying the scenery is definitely one of the best parts of the FFXI adventure! "**... There is no doubt about that (I still feel like doing it sometimes too, haha), but make sure to unlock them so you can warp whenever you need to! ### Priority: "Rhapsodies of Vana'diel" Missions!
Among the many missions, we strongly recommend **prioritizing the "Rhapsodies of Vana'diel" (RoV) missions.** There are two main reasons for this:

1. **Experience Bonuses**: By progressing through the missions and obtaining key items called "Rhapsodies of [Color] Umbra/Radiance," you gain a significant bonus to earned experience points.
2. **Increased Number of Trusts**: Initially, you can only summon 3 Trusts. By progressing through RoV, you can increase this number to 4 and eventually 5. Being able to summon 5 Trusts allows you to form a full party (yourself + 5 Trusts), dramatically increasing your combat strength.

Since reaching the point where you can summon 5 Trusts happens relatively early, make that your first goal.

### Collect "All" Trusts You Can Find
Along with increasing the number of summons, we strongly recommend **obtaining every Trust you can, even those you don't think you'll use.**

- **Condition for Enhancement Quests**: Later on, Eminence Record quests will appear that allow you to enhance Trust stats, and the condition for these quests involves the "number of Trusts obtained."
- **Utilize Login Points**: Currently, many limited-time Trusts can be obtained through "Login Campaign" point exchanges. Be sure to exchange for these without fail.

#### Recommended Trust
In particular, **[Shantotto II](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/34935.html#ShantottoII)** is an extremely powerful ally for leveling. Her powerful magic attacks and Magic Burst (MB) coordination are exceptional, so prioritize obtaining her if the opportunity arises!

"Numbers are strength." Increasing your collection will lead to your eventual personal strength.

### Parallel Progress with Limit Break Quests
"Limit Break Quests" that occur during the leveling process should be progressed in parallel with the main story (especially RoV) for maximum efficiency.

## 2. Leveling and Gil Farming via Eminence Records

One thing you must utilize during leveling is **"Eminence Records."**
By clearing various objectives in the "Quests" menu, you will accumulate "Eminence points" along with experience.

### Use of Eminence Points
Accumulated points can be exchanged for equipment.
- **Use as Your Own Gear**: Excellent as a bridge until you get proper IL gear.
- **Sell to NPCs for Gil**: You can earn the funds (Gil) needed for your adventure by selling unnecessary equipment to NPCs.

### Efficient Gil Farming: Selling Acheron Shields
There is a limit to Eminence exchanges (up to 100,000 points per week), but if you exchange all of these for equipment and sell them to NPCs, you can earn **approximately 1 million Gil per week.**

- **Exchange Ratio**: The sell price to NPCs is 10 times the exchange point value.
- **Recommended Item**: If using for Gil farming, the **"Acheron Shield"** is recommended as it has the highest exchange point value.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Warning**
> Basically, equipment with the "Ex (Exclusive)" property cannot be sold to NPCs. There are some exceptions among Eminence items, but generally, you won't go wrong choosing the Acheron Shield.

### Every Month! Accumulate "Deeds"
Eminence Records include "Monthly" objectives. Clearing these accumulates **"Deeds."**

- **Point Limit**: You can earn a **maximum of 40 Deeds per month.**
- **Why it's important**: Depending on your cumulative total of Deeds, you can obtain extremely powerful equipment, rare items, and enhancement materials as rewards.

Since you cannot earn a large amount at once, be sure to clear the objectives every month and accumulate them steadily!

## 3. Recommended Jobs for Solo Activity

After reaching Level 99, these jobs are particularly recommended for efficient solo movement.

### Thief (THF)
**Primary use: Gil farming, equipment gathering**
- **Treasure Hunter (TH)**: Thief's greatest weapon. It increases drop rates from enemies, dramatically improving the efficiency of gathering equipment (especially the [Malignance set](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/39726.html)).
- **Cornerstone of Gil Farming**: The strategy of gathering "Black Residue" in the Omen "Trash" route, which becomes accessible later, is extremely powerful.

### Blue Mage (BLU)
**Primary use: AOE farming, versatile soloing**
- **AOE Magic Annihilation**: BLU has powerful and MP-efficient AOE spells, ideal for earning Merit Points in a short time.
- **The Learning Curve**: It's a bit tough until you learn (Learning) the spells, but once you have a decent repertoire, it becomes one of the strongest solo jobs capable of both physical and magical combat.

### Red Mage (RDM)
**Primary use: High-difficulty soloing, BF Gil farming**
- **Ultimate Versatility**: Multitalented like Blue Mage, but the hurdle for optimizing gear is slightly higher.
- **Solo High-Tier BFs**: Once geared, RDM can solo High-Tier Mission Battlefields (HTMB) like "The Warrior's Path," enabling Gil farming through the rewards.

## 4. Road to Malignance Gear: Prepare Bridge Gear First

To obtain the [Malignance Armor set](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/39726.html), one of the strongest sets dropped from "The Lady of Wings," you first need to prepare "bridge gear" to challenge it.

### Recommended: "Taeon Armor" First
Gathering **"Taeon Armor"** is an efficient way to bridge the gap until you have Ambuscade gear.
- **Source**: Albion Skirmish - Yorcia Weald [U]
- **Features**: Relatively easy to obtain, and by adding "Augments," the performance becomes viable even in former front-line content.
- **Note**: Albion Skirmish - Yorcia has unique rules, so it may take a little getting used to.

### Ambuscade Gear (Mummu & Ayamo)
For steady solo progress, gear obtained from Ambuscade is also very powerful.
- **Recommended**: **[Mummu Set](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/36746.html)**, **[Ayamo Set](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/36745.html)**
- **Progression**: It's smooth to strengthen your combat power with Taeon gear while accumulating Ambuscade points for exchange. Of course, combining Taeon and Ambuscade is also an option!

Once you've solidified your combat power with these, it's finally time to challenge Lilith in "The Lady of Wings" and aim for the full Malignance set!

## 5. Recommended Weapons: Ambuscade & Oboro Weapons

Once your armor is in place, aim to strengthen your weapons. In particular, "Ambuscade Weapons" can be used even by current front-line players if fully enhanced.

### Essential Ambuscade Weapons
There are stages to enhancement, and gathering materials might be tough at first, but these two are worth prioritizing:

- **[Naegling](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/37597.html) (Sword)**: The weapon skill "Savage Blade" is extremely powerful. This is a god-tier weapon favored by almost all active players.
- **[Tauret](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/37596.html) (Dagger)**: Significantly increases the speed of farming "The Lady of Wings" as a Thief. It's top-class performance until you get a more powerful dagger.

### Bridge to Ambuscade Weapons: "Oboro Job-Specific Gear"
It takes time to fully enhance Ambuscade weapons. As a bridge until then, job-specific weapons from the NPC "Oboro" in Port Jeuno are also recommended.

- **Recommended**: **Sandung (Dagger)**
- **Obtaining**: Relatively easy to get and excellent as a main weapon until your Ambuscade weapon matures.

Refer here for details: **[Oboro Job-Specific Gear](https://wiki.ffo.jp/html/35453.html)**

## 6. Challenging Malignance Gear: The Lady of Wings (Lilith)

Once your bridge gear is ready, it's finally time to challenge the HTMB "The Lady of Wings" for Malignance Armor.

### Recommended Difficulty and Trust Setup
It's recommended to start farming on **"Very Easy (VE)"** first.

- **Recommended Trusts**:
  - **Valaineral** (Tank)
  - **Kupipi** (Healer)
  - **Koru-Moru** (Buffer/Debuffer)
  - **Joachim** (Bard)
  - **Ulmia** (Bard)

### Key to Victory: Avoiding and Luring Traps (Gyve)
This BF has a unique gimmick where fighting near the magical circles (traps) placed on the outer edge results in extra damage or makes it impossible to damage the boss.

Furthermore, Lilith summons **two traps (Gyves)** during combat. Being near these traps results in a "Magic Evasion Down" debuff, so it's important to fight away from summoned traps.

#### Specific Maneuvering Steps
1. **Start Fighting Near the Entrance**: Engage Lilith immediately at the entrance side of the BF.
2. **Let Her Summon Two Traps**: Hold your ground until Lilith places two traps.
3. **Move to the Far Side**: Once two traps are out, move to the far side (beyond the center) of the BF and resume dealing damage.

#### Tips for Moving Trusts
If you are tanking yourself, just move. But if you're letting a Trust (like Valaineral) tank, you need to move them using these steps:

- **Steps**: **Disengage combat** → **Move a large distance** → **Draw weapon and resume attack**
- **Warning**: If you disengage when a Trust's health is low, healing might not keep up. **Ensure Trust health is sufficient before moving.**
- **Stay Calm**: Since traps are summoned twice, you will do this "Disengage → Move" twice. Stay calm and watch your Trusts' status.

### Job Choice and Mindset for Drops
Ideally, use a **Thief** with a high "Treasure Hunter (TH)" rank. However, a very effective alternative for speed is to use **a high-damage physical job with Thief as a support job (Sapo Shi).**

- **Speed-Focused Choices**: BLU/THF, WAR/THF, MNK/THF, etc.
- **Author's Experience**: Often, shortening the time per battle with a high-damage job and doing more repetitions is a faster path to the final drop than fighting slowly as a main Thief.

#### Be Persistent!
Although Malignance Armor is said to drop more easily than before, it's not uncommon to go "100 runs with no drops" if you're unlucky. You might start to doubt if it really drops, but it's a battle of persistence!

It is top-class gear that can be obtained solo without spending Gil, and once you have it, it's a lifelong asset you won't regret. Don't give up!
